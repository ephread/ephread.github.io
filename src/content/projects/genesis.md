I joined the mobile team at Genesis Energy, a New Zealand energy company, to work on Genesis Energy IQ, the billing and energy management app used by its 650,000 customers.

Over eight months, I worked closely with the designer on the data visualizations and charts for Eco Tracker, a new feature that helps customers follow their carbon footprint.

I also designed and built the new REST APIs in the Node.js microservice behind the app, automated code signing and streamlined the Azure CI environment with the tech lead, and overhauled the mobile documentation so knowledge stayed with the team.
