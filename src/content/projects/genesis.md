While working as part of the mobile team at Genesis Energy, I was involved in
the development of the EnergyIQ app. My main responsibilities included implementing
new management features and optimizing existing ones.

During my seven-month tenure, I helped to transform the app from a simple billing tool to
a comprehensive energy management platform, which included advanced features such as
real-time carbon emissions tracking and usage breakdowns.

In addition, I provided valuable support to the backend team by helping to
develop new endpoints to facilitate these improvements.
