As a member of the Genetrainer team, I developed a muscle selection module that allows athletes
to add comments and notes to specific muscle groups and joints. This information can then be
used by trainers and physical therapists to set personalized goals and training plans.

In addition, the module can display the level of fatigue associated with each muscle
and joint after a training session is completed, and can be easily integrated into
a web-centric environment.

While this project may have been small in scope, it provided me with a valuable opportunity
to hone my design and illustration skills, and was an immensely enriching experience overall.
