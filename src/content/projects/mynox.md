Mynöx is a unique social network that caters to private events, allowing users to host events
at their place and invite friends or strangers using the app.

The app is equipped with a wide range of features, such as real-time chat, a Tinder-like
interface for inviting guests based on specific criteria, a notification center to stay
up-to-date on event updates and join requests, as well as Facebook integration for
finding and inviting friends.

I built upon the previous Material Design mockups provided by the client and tailored
them to fit the iOS design language, ensuring a seamless user experience.
