As the creator of Abjapp, an iPhone application designed to teach the Arabic alphabet, I
successfully developed and launched a user-friendly and effective learning tool for
students of all ages.

Through a series of lessons focused on three specific letters at a time, users can progress
through the 28-letter alphabet and validate their knowledge with quizzes.

In addition, Abjapp offers users the ability to practice their knowledge at any time with
randomized quizzes, and provides detailed information about each letter, including
pronunciation and writing techniques for different positions within a word.
