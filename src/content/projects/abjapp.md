Abjapp teaches the Arabic alphabet three letters at a time, with lessons, quizzes and a guide to how each letter is written and pronounced. I designed it from top to bottom, including the teaching material.

Each lesson introduces three new letters and ends with a quiz, so learners work their way through all 28 letters at their own pace.

Randomized quizzes let them practice whenever they like, and every letter has its own page explaining how it sounds and how its shape changes at the start, middle and end of a word.

It’s rated 4.7 out of 5 on average across every country and region, and had more than 850 weekly users in 2023.
